Industry Collaboration Aug 2026

Four-Channel Vision Inspection for Injection Molding

A low-cost conveyor inspection system for injection-molded rubber gaskets, built for an industry partner through the AI-Robotics Volunteer Corps program. Photo-sensor-triggered strobe lighting and four GigE cameras capture every face of each part, and a multi-label ConvNeXt classifier detects five defect types, reaching an F1-score of 0.968 at 60 parts per minute. I designed and built the camera and lighting rig and the aluminum-profile frame.

Coursework Mar – Jun 2023

Mechanical System Design & Robot Programming

A freshman mechanical engineering course at SNU covering Fusion 360 CAD from engineering drawings, Python and MATLAB exercises such as RANSAC fitting and k-means clustering, and ROS with computer vision. In the final team project, a ROS vision node read red or blue signals from a camera to set a propeller's spin direction, and the team designed and 3D-printed the propellers.
